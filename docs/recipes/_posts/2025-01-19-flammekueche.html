--- layout: recipe title: "Flammekueche" tags: dish alsacian author: Gogo preview_picture: "/assets/images/flammekueche.jpg" ingredients: servings: 4 list: - name: flour quantity: 250 unit: g - name: warm water quantity: 12 unit: cL - name: neutral oil (sunflower or rapeseed) quantity: 5 unit: cL - name: salt - name: thick cream quantity: 20 unit: g - name: cottage cheese quantity: 20 unit: g - name: lardon quantity: 200 unit: g - name: onion quantity: 2 --- Temps de préparation 30 min, temps de cuisson 15 min

Instructions

  1. In a mixing bowl, mix the flour and salt and make a well in the middle.
  2. Add the oil and water.
  3. With your hands knead to have a smooth dough. Rest it for 15 min.
  4. In the mean time pre-cook the lardons in the microwave at 700/900W for 4/3min to drain the water.
  5. Finely slice the onions.
  6. In a large bowl, mix the cream and cream cheese. Ideally, beat together with a mixer to obtain a creamier flammekueche.
  7. Preheat your oven at maximum heat.
  8. Roll out the dough. It should covera large baking sheet and not be too thick.
  9. Spread the cream/cheese mixture. Then add the lardons and onions.
  10. Cook for 15, 12 or 10 min if your heat is 220, 250 or 300°C
Can be serve as appetizers or main dish with a salad
